Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 November 2026
Operated by Praise Osabuohien, Nigeria

This Privacy Policy explains how Praise Osabuohien (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you use SoftPraise Analytics (the “App”). The App is a personal tool for the owner of TikTok account @softpraise0901 to view their own profile and video statistics. It is not a public analytics service, and it does not sell data.

By using the App, you agree to this policy. For our rules of use, see the Terms of Service.

1. Who is responsible

Praise Osabuohien, based in Nigeria, operates SoftPraise Analytics and is the person you should contact about this policy. Email: praiseog91@gmail.com.

2. Information we access from TikTok

When you connect your TikTok account, the App requests only the following read-only scopes. We receive the information TikTok makes available under each scope, which typically includes:

  • user.info.basic — basic identity such as your TikTok open ID, display name, and avatar.
  • user.info.profile — profile details such as your username, bio, and profile image.
  • user.info.stats — account statistics such as follower count, following count, and likes.
  • video.list — a list of your videos and their public stats, such as titles or captions, cover images, create time, and view, like, comment, and share counts, as provided by TikTok.

We do not ask for permission to post, comment, message, or change your account. The exact fields can change if TikTok updates its APIs.

3. Other information we may collect

If you email us, we will have your email address and the content of your message. The App or its hosting provider may also keep ordinary technical logs (such as IP address, browser type, and time of visit) needed to run and secure the site. We do not use advertising cookies or sell analytics about visitors.

4. How we use the information

TikTok data is used only to show the account owner their own analytics — for example, current follower totals and how each video is performing. We also use information to:

  • Sign you in securely and keep your session working
  • Respond to questions, support requests, and deletion requests
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the App
  • Comply with law if we are legally required to do so

We do not use your TikTok data to train AI models, to advertise to you or others, or to build profiles about other people.

5. What we do not do

We do not sell your information. We do not share it with third parties for their marketing. We do not give other TikTok users access to your stats through this App.

6. Storage and retention

Profile and video statistics may be stored temporarily so the App can display them and so daily or weekly check-ins can compare recent changes. Access tokens are stored only as needed to keep the connection working, through Composio as described below.

We keep this data only while the TikTok account stays connected and the data is still needed for those purposes. When you revoke access or ask us to delete your data, we delete stored TikTok data and tokens within 30 days, except for information we must keep to meet a legal obligation or to resolve a dispute. Technical logs are kept only for a short period unless a security issue requires a longer review.

7. Third parties

These parties may process information so the App can function:

  • TikTok. Sign-in and data access happen through TikTok Login Kit and TikTok’s APIs. TikTok handles your account under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not control TikTok.
  • Composio. Composio is an OAuth integration platform that helps the App complete TikTok sign-in and retrieve the authorized data. Composio may process access tokens and API responses as part of that connection, under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Hosting and email providers. The company that hosts this website, and the provider of our contact email, may process technical or message data as needed to deliver those services.

We only use these parties to operate the App. We do not authorize them to use your TikTok analytics for their own unrelated purposes.

8. Your rights

You can:

  • Review the stats the App shows about your account, which come from TikTok
  • Revoke the App’s access at any time in your TikTok account settings (for example, under connected apps or similar privacy controls)
  • Ask us what data we hold, or ask us to correct or delete it

Depending on where you live, including under applicable Nigerian data-protection law, you may also have rights to object to or restrict certain processing, or to lodge a complaint with a relevant authority. To use these rights, email praiseog91@gmail.com. We may need to confirm that the request comes from the account owner.

9. Deletion requests

To request deletion of data stored by SoftPraise Analytics, email praiseog91@gmail.com with the subject “Deletion request”. We will confirm and delete stored TikTok data and tokens as described in the retention section above. You should also revoke the App’s access in your TikTok settings so TikTok stops sharing new data. Deleting data from this App does not delete your TikTok account or your videos on TikTok.

10. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. International processing

We are based in Nigeria. TikTok, Composio, and our hosting or email providers may process information on servers in other countries. If you connect your account, you understand that your information may be transferred to and stored in those locations, with the safeguards those providers apply.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date on this page. If a change is material, we will try to give reasonable notice, for example by a note on the App or by email if we have one for you. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, access requests, or deletion requests, contact Praise Osabuohien at praiseog91@gmail.com.
