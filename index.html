Your own TikTok stats, in one place

SoftPraise Analytics is a personal tool for the owner of the TikTok account @softpraise0901. It shows that account’s profile and video statistics and nothing else.

What the app does

After you sign in with TikTok, the app reads your public profile details and video list so you can see follower counts, likes, and how each of your videos is performing. Access is read-only. The app does not post, comment, message, or change anything on your account.

  • View your profile information and avatar
  • See follower, following, and like totals
  • Browse your own video list and public video stats

Data is used only to show you your own analytics. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Connect with TikTok

The app uses TikTok Login Kit so only the account owner can sign in. Access is handled through Composio, an OAuth integration platform. You will be sent to TikTok to approve these read-only permissions:

  • user.info.basic — basic account identity
  • user.info.profile — your profile details
  • user.info.stats — follower, following, and like counts
  • video.list — a list of your videos and their public stats
  1. Open SoftPraise Analytics and choose to connect your TikTok account.
  2. Sign in on TikTok, if you are not already signed in, and review the requested permissions.
  3. Approve access. TikTok then returns you to the app with a secure token, via Composio.
  4. Your profile and video stats appear on screen. You can revoke access at any time in your TikTok settings.

Connect with TikTok

The “Connect with TikTok” step is available inside the app after TikTok approves this developer application. It is not a public sign-up. This site explains the product for review and for the account owner.
